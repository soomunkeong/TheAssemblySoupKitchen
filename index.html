THE ASSEMBLY SOUP KITCHEN
ABOUT US

Doing what we can,
with what we have.

The Assembly Soup Kitchen (T.A.S.K) started with a handful of volunteers cooking food from their own kitchens — doing what they could with what they had.

We are an independent, all-volunteer organisation that serves the homeless and hungry predominantly throughout the Klang Valley.

We rely on volunteers, local businesses and the generosity of the community to continue our mission of providing hot, nutritious meals to those in need.

Our vision

To provide food security to Orang Asli, refugee, B40 and other underserved communities throughout the Klang Valley.

WHAT WE DO

More than a meal.

Our work extends beyond food. Through our programmes, we seek to create opportunities, provide education and empower communities.

01

TASK E.A.T.S

Employment & Training Scheme

Empowering refugee mothers to become more independent and confident through practical training and opportunities.

Training includes baking, cooking, financial literacy, management and leadership skills.

02

TASK Angels

Empowering young people

TASK Angels creates a safe and supportive environment for teens and young adults with special needs and high-functioning autism.

Activities include simple baking, music, educational trips and life skills.

03

Assembly Learning Center

Education & opportunity

The Assembly Learning Center was established to meet the educational needs of children in the refugee community.

From 2022–2025, we supported the educational needs of more than 300 children from Grades 1–9.
